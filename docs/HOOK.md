# HOOK AND VARIETY PLAN (from real playtests, overrides anything softer)

## Diagnosis (what the maintainer saw, as a player)
1. Every stage is the same loop: find the key, code or battery, open the door. Nothing NEW arrives after stage 3.
2. Every room reuses the same platform layout ("the same slab in every room").
3. The ghost cannot hurt anyone yet, so there is no tension. At stage 25 it is still the same small cute Wisp.
4. The light flicker is unexplained, so it feels random instead of scary.
5. "It gets interesting at stage 50" is fatal. A new player decides in the first 10 to 15 minutes.

## Principles
1. **Novelty cadence:** something NEW every stage for stages 1 to 15 (a mechanic, a ghost behaviour, a lock, a hazard), then every 2 to 3 stages.
   No stage repeats the previous stage's room template or lock type. Hard requirement, tested.
2. **Front-load the fun:** by stage 5 a player has met the ghost, hiding, the Pulse Gun, a two-person lock and a darkness twist.
3. **Stakes first:** judge the game only after catch, rescue and the gun exist (P3E steps 3 and 4).
4. **Variety budget:** at least 12 room templates (corridor, shaft, split level, mezzanine, vent crawl, moving platforms, pit, tower, hub), at least 4 visual
   themes (palette, props, ambient sound: lab, hospital, factory, library), and a puzzle pool of at least 12 types. The generator draws from them with
   "newness weighting" so recent choices are avoided.
5. **Choice and reward every stage:** draft pick (1 of 3 upgrades: flashlight range, quieter steps, extra slot, faster rescue, cheaper stun...), an optional
   loot room (risk and reward), optional Risk Contracts. Rewards persist for the run, some meta unlocks arrive with accounts (P6).
6. **The ghost evolves in LOOK, SOUND and BEHAVIOUR:** look tiers by stage (the stage 25 Wisp is not the stage 2 Wisp: bigger, trailing, distorted), the types
   from GAMEPLAY.md at their stages, and a mini-boss variant on every 10th stage.
7. **Explain every cue the first time:** the first warning shows one line ("The lights flicker. Something is near."). Teach before test.
8. **Short feedback loops:** a new idea, a scare or a reward at least every 60 to 90 seconds of play.

## Curriculum v1 (stages 1 to 15; the generator follows it, then switches to weighted variety)
| Stage | New thing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tutorial: locker key, door, exit | no ghost, container glow, high light |
| 2 | First ghost (Wisp) and the flicker warning | hiding spot taught with a prompt |
| 3 | Code lock and notes, a decoy note | noise lesson: running is loud, Shift sneaks |
| 4 | Pulse Gun in a safe | stun the ghost, shrinking stun |
| 5 | Two-person lock (clone or timed alternative for solo), 2 rooms | co-op moment |
| 6 | Battery and power lock: carry a battery across a ghost-patrolled room | 2-slot decisions |
| 7 | Shade ghost (frozen by your flashlight) | new counter |
| 8 | Vertical puzzle with a gravity pickup | abilities return as rewards |
| 9 | Lights-out room, flare item | darkness twist |
| 10 | MAJOR BREACH: 3 rooms, mini-boss Wisp, draft pick plus new flashlight, short celebration | milestone |
| 11 | Lever-sequence lock and a hazard (sparks) | |
| 12 | Howler ghost (scream breaks lights) | crouch counter |
| 13 | Optional loot room (risk and reward) | |
| 14 | Mirror/symbol-matching puzzle across two rooms (talk to your team) | co-op information |
| 15 | First disaster layer with an optional cure | relief reward |

## What changes in the roadmap
P3E continues (steps 3 to 5). Then **P3F = Variety and Hook** (curriculum, room templates and themes, ghost look tiers, puzzle pool, draft pick,
loot room, first-warning explanation), BEFORE disasters, chat or death. Everything else follows after a playtest with 2 to 3 friends.
