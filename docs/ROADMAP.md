|---|---|

| **P5A/B** | Scenario model, validator, deterministic fallback, then OpenAI generation |
| **P6** | Accounts, save, guests, progression |
| **P7** | Polish, E2E, security, performance, deployment, submission |

After P3E and after P4: a 20-minute playtest with friends who have never seen the game.
