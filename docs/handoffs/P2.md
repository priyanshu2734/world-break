(g) completion goes through the existing `STAGE_CLEAR` transition; (h) a mitigation is never "resolved"; (i) power objectives count grid power only, then seal their breakers.

## 6. Known bugs and incomplete work
- No replay after `stage_complete` (reload the page). `game:reset` only works during play; there is no `playing`-> or `stage_complete`->lobby path from the client.
- Deadline is **soft**: overrun drains score, it does not fail the stage. Hard failure / team wipe / death review are P4.
- Hazards cost score, interrupt channels and set progress back; they do not move players or damage health (no health model). Movement-affecting disasters need a `sim.ts` migration (P3).
- Tools are per player with no transfer; the supply cache refills at a score cost. Anything held by a disconnected player is simply gone until reset.
- Hazard hits occur if a player walks through the armed arc zone before shielding (observed: -75 to -225 in browser runs). Intended consequence, but not telegraphed before the objective unlocks.
- The route to the core deck needs the middle-to-upper-left jump (no time pressure). Phase locks A, B and C are on L1, centre deck and floor.
- `stage:state` is re-sent every tick during a channel (~1-2 KB at 20 Hz): fine for 4 players, not optimised.
- Rate limiter counts refused actions too (3 ticks per player).
- `?renderer=canvas` and `__WB_DEBUG__` (incl. `setInput`) exist for verification; the hook is dev-build only.
- Layout nits: the other player's name label can sit under the tool bar; breakpoint and player labels overlap when stacked.
- Operational gotcha: starting servers via `npm run` and killing by wrapper PID leaves the node child running and silently serves an OLD stage. Check `ss -ltn | grep 3001` before verifying.
